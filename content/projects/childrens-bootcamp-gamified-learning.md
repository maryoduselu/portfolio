## What I did

I design instructional slides and visual learning materials for the children's online classes. I use the ADDIE framework to analyse what learners need, develop the content and evaluate how well each lesson worked.

For the church's yearly children's bootcamp (ages 5 to 12), I gamified the learning resources and anchored the live game sessions. I also coordinate a team of volunteers so that the content is delivered the same way in every session.
